How it works
  1. Your browser asks for notification permission.
  2. The service worker at /sw.js is registered (must be same-origin).
  3. The app subscribes with the server’s VAPID public key and saves your subscription under the provided siteId.
  4. When a message is sent to that siteId, the service worker shows the notification and opens the URL on click.
Notes
  • Works on secure origins (HTTPS) and on http://localhost for local testing.
  • To send messages, use the server’s POST /api/notify. If an API key is configured, include Authorization: Bearer <key>.
  • Unreachable endpoints are cleaned up automatically by the server.
